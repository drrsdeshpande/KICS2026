HAPE Pathology & Management: Immediate descent & Oxygenation

Pathophysiology & Management

High Altitude Pulmonary Edema (HAPE): Exaggerated hypoxic pulmonary vasoconstriction leads to uneven capillary hydrostatic stress failure and high-permeability non-cardiogenic edema. Definitive management: immediate descent (>1000m), supplemental $O_2$, hyperbaric bag (Gamow bag), and nifedipine / phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitors.
